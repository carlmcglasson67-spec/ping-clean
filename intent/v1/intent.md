# Intent: Ping Clean V1

## What we're building
A marketplace that lets Cumbria Bright's marketing generate unlimited regular domestic
cleans without Cumbria Bright employing more staff. Customers book online at a fixed
price; vetted self-employed cleaners claim the work; Ping keeps a commission.

## Why it wins
- Quality is enforced: mandatory checklist and photos on every clean, cleaners ranked on results.
- Good cleaners get first pick: Top tier sees new jobs first.
- Customers keep their cleaner: a regular clean is claimed as a series.
- Built by cleaners: every screen quicker than a WhatsApp message.

## Success for V1
A handful of Cumbria Bright regulars and 2–3 trusted cleaners complete two weeks of
visits on Ping with no admin workarounds.

## Not V1
Native apps, extras (ovens, ironing), vouchers, blog engine, CRM, multi-company SaaS.
Rule: nothing joins V1 after sign-off without dropping something of equal size.

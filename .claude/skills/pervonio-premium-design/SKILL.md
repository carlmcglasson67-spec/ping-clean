# Pervonio premium design

Standard: master-craftsman, "Rolls-Royce" quality. Every detail considered, down to the
last full stop on every page. Apply from the first commit, never as a fix-up later.

## Marketing site (app/(site))
- Never squash the hero into a narrow centred column with dead space either side or
  content tight under the header. Use generous full-width editorial spacing, asymmetry,
  scale and original pacing. Benchmarks: DEPT, AKQA, ustwo, Code and Theory.
- No plain blocks of text floating on white. Every section has structure: imagery,
  contrast panels, numbers, or layout that carries the eye.
- No small or cheap-looking type. Large confident display type, comfortable body
  (18px+ on desktop), real type scale.
- Depth to match the Lakeland benchmark (lakelandliv.vercel.app): mega-menu nav, hero
  with image tiles, big stats, before/after block with numbered steps, deep area and
  service pages, FAQ on home. Deliver that depth in one pass.

## Apps (app/(account), app/(work), app/(admin))
- Speed beats decoration. The cleaner's main actions (claim, start, tick, photo,
  complete) are one thumb tap each, 48px+ targets, usable with wet hands in bad signal.
- Every screen has designed empty, loading and error states. No raw error text.
- Same tokens and type as the site so it feels like one brand.

## In this repo: the Ping Clean brand
Use the tokens, logo components and rules in the Brand section of CLAUDE.md. Navy and
blue must feel premium, not generic tech: deep navy, generous space, fine signal-wave
lines, big confident type, one bright action per view. Take the flyers' palette, logo
and mood, never their busy layouts.

## Copy
British English. Plain, warm, specific. No filler, no lorem ipsum, no "Welcome to our website".

Before reporting done, screenshot the page at 390px and 1440px wide and check it against
every rule above.
